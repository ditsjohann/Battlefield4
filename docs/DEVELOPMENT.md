# WARFRONT — Development Log & Plan

Living document: current direction, what works, what's in progress, known issues and next
priorities. Update it as the project evolves rather than rewriting it.

## Direction

**"Conquest anywhere."** WARFRONT turns your own Minecraft world into a combined-arms
battlefield. A War Table deploys a full Conquest battle onto the terrain in front of it: two
fortified HQs, a zig-zag line of capture points with sandbag fortifications, armour pads and a
destructible Comms Tower. Two AI armies (the **Iron Coalition** and the **Ember Legion**) fight
over the flags in squads directed by a commander AI, and you enlist on one side. You pick a kit,
lead an AI fireteam, and win by bleeding the enemy's tickets.

Design pillars, taken from the BF4 research (see `RESEARCH.md`):
1. **Conquest is the loop.** Flags give gunfights a purpose: spawning, ticket bleed, territory.
2. **Readable ballistic gunplay.** Spread, bloom, recoil, falloff, velocity and drop all follow
   BF4's documented weapon model.
3. **Class interdependence.** Revives, ammo, repairs and spotting make teammates matter.
4. **Combined arms.** Tanks need engineers; engineers need cover; infantry need tanks to break
   lines.
5. **Destruction changes the map.** Craters, shattered glass, punched-through planks, crushed
   fences, and the Levolution tower collapse that permanently changes the centre flag.

## Architecture

```
com.ditsjohann.warfront
├── Warfront, WarfrontConfig        mod entry, server config (destruction, AI counts, tickets…)
├── registry/                       DeferredRegisters: items, blocks, BEs, entities, sounds, components, tab
├── weapon/                         GunType (stats), Attachment/Loadout, EffectiveStats, GunItem,
│                                   GunLogic (server fire/reload/rate-limit), BulletManager (ballistics)
├── combat/                         Faction/Teams, CombatEvents (hits, downed rule, kill feed, assists),
│                                   Suppression, Spotting, DownedState, Explosions
├── entity/                         SoldierEntity + ai/ goals, TankEntity, Rocket, FragGrenade, C4, SupplyBag, SpawnBeacon
├── item/                           gadget items (charges in data components)
├── block/                          War Table, Capture Point (+BE), HQ (+BE), Resupply Crate, materials
├── match/                          ConquestMatch, Objective, Commander, MatchManager (SavedData),
│                                   BattlefieldGenerator, Deployment, Kit, TowerCollapse, MatchSnapshot
├── progression/                    Ranks, PlayerProgress (persistent data), Scoring
├── network/                        one SimpleChannel; records with StreamCodecs
├── client/                         GunController, HudRenderer, ClientState, screens, renderers, AutoTest
└── gametest/                       in-world automated tests
```

Key decisions (with reasoning in `RESEARCH.md` §3):
- **Bullets are not entities.** `BulletManager` sweeps a raycast per tick per round (gravity,
  drag, penetration, suppression). Clients get one tracer packet and re-simulate the visual.
- **Client-side recoil, server-side authority.** The client applies camera recoil and spread and
  sends the final aim; the server checks ammo, reload state, a token-bucket fire rate and aim
  plausibility.
- **No external dependencies.** All animation is procedural (first-person gun transforms,
  box-built tank and flags) and all assets are generated by scripts in `tools/`.
- **Data components** hold all per-gun state (ammo, reserve, reload timer, fire mode,
  attachments). Defaults attach lazily because vanilla registers component types *after* items.
- **Boat-style vehicle sync.** The tank is client-driven for its driver and server-driven for
  AI crews.

## Feature status

### Done (vertical slice)
- **Weapons:** 8 guns (AR, alt-AR, carbine, LMG, bolt sniper, DMR, pump shotgun, pistol). Fire
  modes (auto/burst/semi/bolt), ADS with per-optic zoom and sensitivity scaling, bloom/decay
  spread, first-shot recoil, damage falloff, headshots, bullet drop, tracers, muzzle flash,
  layered near/far gunfire audio, reloads (tactical vs empty), hit markers (hit/headshot/kill).
- **Attachments:** 5 optics (with distinct 3D models), suppressor/compensator/heavy barrel,
  vertical/angled grip, bipod. Rank-gated and validated server-side.
- **Bullet/world interaction:** glass/ice/leaves shatter, rounds penetrate planks/wool/doors,
  shotguns breach doors, sandbags stop rounds, impact particles and ricochets.
- **AI soldiers:** 4 roles, faction uniforms, squad assignment, target selection, reaction time,
  burst discipline, lead and drop compensation, half-cover seeking under suppression, peeking,
  grenades on hidden targets, engineer rockets vs armour, medics revive downed allies and players,
  recon spotting, waypointed long-range movement, flag holding.
- **Conquest:** capture/neutralise with numbers advantage, ticket bleed by flag majority, ticket
  loss on casualties and refund on revive, reinforcement waves, per-team Commander assigning
  squads to flags, base protection, low-ticket warnings, victory/defeat, cleanup, persistence.
- **Battlefield generator:** HQs, fortified flags, resupply crates, vehicle pads, the Comms Tower.
- **Player loop:** War Table (start/join/leave/end), deploy screen (kit, primary, attachments,
  tactical-map spawn: HQ / flag / beacon / squad), kits with gadgets, downed state with
  bleed-out/give-up, personal AI fireteam that follows you or attacks/defends a flag you order.
- **Gadgets:** frag grenade, medic bag, ammo bag, defibrillator, RL-4 rocket launcher, repair
  tool (heat), C4 (place, stick to vehicles, remote det), spawn beacon, binocular spotting,
  sandbags.
- **Tank:** drivable MBT with turret and gun following view, cannon (reload), coax MG, armour
  model, disabled/burning state, repair, crushing soft blocks, roadkills, AI crews, pad respawns.
- **Levolution:** tower integrity damaged by base explosions → alarm → topples into falling
  blocks toward the centre flag; destroys the radar uplink, which auto-spots enemies for whoever
  holds the tower flag.
- **Feedback & HUD:** ammo panel, health, dynamic crosshair, scope overlay, conquest bar, capture
  status, rotating minimap (flags, HQs, friendlies, spotted/revealed enemies), kill feed, score
  popups, announcements, suppression/damage vignettes, camera shake, tank HUD, scoreboard.
- **Progression:** 14 ranks, XP from all score events, persistent career stats, rank-gated unlocks.

### Done (second wave)
- **Multiplayer polish:** other players see you shoulder guns in a two-handed aiming pose
  (`IClientItemExtensions#getArmPose`); **scope glint**, where aiming a 4x or 8x optic flashes a
  glint to anyone the scope points at (players via a server-relayed aim state, AI recon snipers
  from their synced aiming flag). It scales with distance so countersnipers can find you.
- **Weapon mastery:** attachments unlock per weapon by kills with that gun (BF4-style), validated
  server-side on deploy. Unlock announcements, and "Next: Holo 2x (4/10)" progress on the deploy
  screen.
- **Squad orders radial (commo rose):** hold V for attack/defend on each flag, follow me, hold
  here, need medic, need ammo. Medic/ammo calls send the nearest friendly AI medic or support
  gunner running over to drop a bag at your feet.
- **Vehicles:** `VehicleEntity` base class (armour model, disabled/burning, seats, boat-style
  sync, crushing, AI driving). New **FAV-3 Jackal** fast attack vehicle: driver plus a rear HMG
  gunner, overheat, thin armour (rifles hurt it), circle-strafing AI. Climb into an AI buggy and
  you take the wheel while the AI crew mans the gun. HQs now field a tank pad and a buggy pad with AI
  crews.
- **AI vehicle tactics:** tanks keep a standoff from infantry (rockets) and hold to shoot; light
  vehicles circle targets; damaged vehicles retreat to HQ, where the ground crew repairs them;
  crews bail out of burning vehicles and fight on foot.
- **Second Levolution set-piece, the fuel depot:** two storage tanks beside flag B. Explosive
  damage ignites a chain reaction (three blasts, crater, fires) that blankets the flag in smoke
  for 45 s.
- **Smoke as a gameplay system:** smoke blocks AI line of sight, spotting and vehicle targeting.
  Recon carries smoke grenades.
- **Sound pass:** three variants of every gunshot (near and far), bullet crack, impact and
  explosion; a low-ammo magazine rattle.

### Testing (verified 2026-11-26)
- **Dedicated server:** Forge's `server_gametest` launch target runs as `Dist.DEDICATED_SERVER`
  (client classes stripped), so the GameTest suite also proves the mod loads and runs on the
  dedicated-server distribution.
- `./gradlew runGameTestServer`: **23/23 pass** (second wave added: buggy armour, crew bail-out,
  weapon-kill unlock rules, smoke blinding AI, fuel depot chain reaction).
- `./gradlew runGameTestServer` (first wave): 18 tests. Covers ballistics, penetration, sandbag cover,
  headshots, pass-through allies, spread cone maths, reload, frag grenade, rocket vs tank, armour
  model, crushability, flag capture, contested flags, ticket bleed, downed/revive ticket
  bookkeeping, tower collapse and attachment trade-offs.
- `WARFRONT_AUTOTEST=1 xvfb-run ./gradlew runClient` (Mesa software GL): full scripted session
  with no crashes. It generates a 5-flag battlefield, deploys, aims, fires, watches the AI war,
  rides and fires the tank, collapses the tower and saves 13 screenshots to `run/screenshots/`.
  Telemetry from the last run (~2 min of battle): both armies at 12-13 soldiers, flags trading
  hands (B: neutral → Coalition → Legion), tickets 150:150 → 85:125 from casualties plus bleed,
  medics reviving downed soldiers, AI-crewed tanks deploying from pads.
- Bugs found by these runs and fixed: AI squads pruned on creation (soldiers idled), far side of
  the battlefield unloading, out-of-bounds gun models, item atlas texture, minimap mirroring,
  revive-goal NPE, bullet penetration re-hitting the same block, oversized head hitbox.

## Known issues / limitations
- First-person arm placement is approximated (procedural, no keyframes).
- AI pathfinding can't plan through blocks it would need to break; soldiers sometimes bunch on
  steep terrain. Waypointing mitigates long routes.
- Tank movement is arcade kinematics; no slope tilt of the hull.
- Downed players are invulnerable until revived or they bleed out (matches BF4); there is no
  finisher.
- A networked dedicated server with two real clients hasn't been exercised: dedicated-dist loading
  is proven via the GameTest server, but `./gradlew runServer` needs `run/eula.txt` accepted by you.
- With the default GUI scale at 720p the HUD is dense; the kill feed is drawn at 75% scale.

## Development environment notes
- ForgeGradle 7's Mavenizer runs tools on **JDK 25** and **JDK 8**. On machines without internet
  access to foojay, install them and export `JAVA_HOME_25_X64` / `JAVA_HOME_8_X64`; also add both
  to `org.gradle.java.installations.paths` in `gradle.properties` if needed.
- Asset pipeline: `python3 tools/gen_textures.py && python3 tools/gen_sounds.py &&
  python3 tools/gen_assets.py && python3 tools/gen_structures.py` (needs numpy, Pillow, ffmpeg).
